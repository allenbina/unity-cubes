# Beats 'n' Cubes "Welcome" video: scenes and phases

Source video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zjs24FUrprg ("Etienne de Crecy - Welcome"), 5:31, 640x480, 25 fps.
It is a short documentary-style clip of the **original 2007 show** (end card reads "TRANSMUSICALES 12/7/2007 RENNES"). It is shot with a roaming camera: front-on, from above looking down into the cube, tilted, and close-ups. So part of what changes between scenes is the camera, and part is the projection.

Scene boundaries were supplied by the user (about 1 to 2 seconds accurate). The footage is only in the git-ignored `reference/` folder.
**These notes come from four frames sampled per scene. I have not heard the audio and I only see stills, so motion and timing are inferred.**

Assumptions: each line of the user's list is one scene (":31, 52" = 0:31 to 0:52). Gaps between scenes are left as listed (1:34-1:39, 2:11-2:14, 2:53-2:54). The list now ends at 5:09; the video runs to 5:31.

## Before the scenes (0:00 to 0:31)
- 0:00 to about 0:20: a white wireframe cube on black, a 3x3x3 grid of lines that rotates and shows its subdivisions. It is the graphic idea of the show before any real footage.
- About 0:20: a title card "ETIENNE DE CRECY".

## The structure being lit
- A 3x3 grid of boxes (nine blocks) in a frame of scaffolding tubes, covered in translucent fabric. The center cell is open and holds the DJ, who shows as a dark silhouette.
- The projection is **lines**: it lights up the actual tube edges of the scaffold, so the picture is an outline of the structure itself, with brighter junction points where tubes meet. Then it adds virtual boxes, planes and grids on top, and they read as 3D because of the fabric layers.
- Palettes: red, then cool white-cyan for most of the show, then green, blue-violet, magenta, cyan and flat red/blue near the end.

## Patterns that look reusable
1. **The lines trace the real structure.** The picture is mostly outlines of tubes and boxes, so it reads as architecture lighting up.
2. **A small vocabulary with variations:** outline the grid; flood a cell solid; slide or extrude boxes; build the grid cell by cell; breathe in scale; subdivide into smaller cubes; dissolve to sparse lines and darkness.
3. **Dynamics come from darkness:** scenes start from black, build, and drop back to black. The DJ reveal in scene 4 is a single big white flash.
4. **Color is used sparingly:** red at the start, then cool white for most of the show, then blue, purple and green near the end.
5. **The camera is part of the show,** at least in this edit: front-on, from above, tilted and close-ups.
